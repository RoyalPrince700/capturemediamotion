## VO Generation Tips

- **Voice:** a rich, warm Nigerian or neutral-African voice works best. On ElevenLabs, try "Adam", "Brian", or search community voices for "Nigerian".
- **Speed:** 1.0×–1.05× — it's a short, punchy read.
- **Naira amounts:** already spelled out ("fifteen thousand naira") so TTS won't trip on "₦".
- **After generating:** save the file as `voiceover.mp3` inside the `public/` folder, then uncomment the voice-over `<Audio>` tag at the bottom of `src/ConvoPromo.tsx`.
